## Transportation 
Traveling to Kuala Lumpur can be an exciting adventure, but navigating a new city may feel a little overwhelming, especially if it's your first visit. Fortunately, Kuala Lumpur is well equipped for international travelers. At Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA), you'll find clear signage in both English and Malay directing you to immigration, baggage claim, taxis, ride-hailing pickup areas, airport trains, buses, and other transportation options.

If you're ever unsure where to go, don't hesitate to ask airport staff or stop by an information counter. They're accustomed to assisting visitors from around the world and are happy to point you in the right direction.

Tip: Save your hotel name and address on your phone (or keep a printed copy with you). It's especially helpful when using taxis or ride-hailing services like Grab, which is the most commonly used ride-hailing app in Malaysia.

## Airport Transportation :airplane:
#### **Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA)**

Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) is the primary international gateway to Malaysia and serves the vast majority of international and domestic flights. Located approximately 55 km (34 miles) from the city center, the airport is modern, easy to navigate, and well-signposted in both English and Malay.

Travelers have several convenient transportation options from the airport, including Grab (Malaysia's most popular ride-hailing service), taxis, airport buses, rental cars, and the KLIA Ekspres train, which reaches Kuala Lumpur Sentral Station in approximately 28 minutes. From there, you can connect to the city's rail network or continue to your hotel by Grab or taxi.

The airport offers a wide range of traveler amenities, including restaurants, cafés, currency exchange, ATMs, SIM card vendors, luggage storage, and free Wi-Fi, making it easy to get settled before heading into the city.

### Train :train:

**The KLIA Ekspres** is the fastest and most convenient way to travel between Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) and the city center. The non-stop train reaches KL Sentral Station in approximately 28 minutes, with departures every 15–20 minutes during most of the day.

Tickets can be purchased online, at station ticket counters, or from self-service ticket machines. Once at KL Sentral, you can easily connect to Kuala Lumpur's rail network or take a Grab or taxi to your hotel.

Website: https://www.kliaekspres.com 

**Kuala Lumpur Rail Network (MRT, LRT & Monorail)**
Kuala Lumpur has an extensive rail network operated by Rapid KL, making it easy to travel throughout the city. The MRT, LRT, and Monorail systems connect many popular destinations, shopping districts, hotels, and business areas.

Tickets can be purchased at station ticket machines, or you can pay using contactless bank cards or mobile payment at many stations. Rapid KL's journey planner can help you determine the best route.

Website: https://myrapid.com.my.


### Bus :bus:
**Rapid KL Buses**
Rapid KL operates an extensive network of public buses serving Kuala Lumpur and neighboring communities. Many routes connect directly with MRT, LRT, and Monorail stations, making it easy to continue your journey throughout the city.

Website: https://myrapid.com.my

**Aerobus**
For those wishing to take the bus between the airport and the city, Aerobus offers a comprehensive schedule connecting KL Sentral with both KLIA1 and KLA2 (see https://www.aerobus.com.my/bus-schedules)

Website: https://www.aerobus.com.my/


### Taxi and Rideshare :taxi::car:
For many IETF attendees, Grab is the easiest way to travel around Kuala Lumpur. Grab is Malaysia's most popular ride-hailing service, offering reliable, cashless transportation throughout the city and from the airport. Taxis are also readily available at designated taxi stands at KLIA and throughout Kuala Lumpur.

## Money Conversion :dollar:

The official currency of Malaysia is the Malaysian Ringgit (MYR). Credit and debit cards are widely accepted at hotels, restaurants, shopping centers, and most businesses throughout Kuala Lumpur. ATMs are readily available at the airport, shopping malls, and throughout the city if you need to withdraw cash.

Electrical Plug and Voltage Information for Malaysia: (https://www.power-plugs-sockets.com/my/malaysia/) 

In Malaysia, power plugs and sockets (outlets) of type G are used. The standard voltage is 240 V at a frequency of 50 Hz.

## Emergency Assistance :hospital:
In the event of an emergency anywhere in Malaysia, dial 999 for police, fire, or ambulance services. If you're calling from a mobile phone and are unable to connect to 999, you can also dial 112, which will route you to emergency services.
