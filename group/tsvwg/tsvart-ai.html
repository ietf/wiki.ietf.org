Guidance for Tool-Assisted Reviews by Directorate Reviewers

This wiki entry provides draft guidance for TSV-ART Reviewers (Nov 2026).

 

Tool-Assisted Reviews

A “review tool” is any technology that can generate human-like prose, produce technical sounding analysis, or otherwise create content that may resemble expert commentary without possessing the underlying expertise, experience, or judgment. This can be an AI system trained on a large collection of text, such as a Large Language Model (LLM), but is not limited to one specific type of “review tool” since the issues described in this statement apply to any automatic generation of text.

Acceptable Use of Tools In Reviews 

The following are some of the uses of tools in preparing reviews that are considered acceptable, provided that the reviewer performed the technical analysis themselves:

  • Checking grammar, spelling, or formatting of the reviewer's own already written analysis
  • Rephrasing the reviewer's own text for clarity or conciseness
  • Translating the reviewer's own analysis from their native language to English
  • Automating research and compilation of sources that are then as rigorously validated as input to human consideration as any other source of researched information
  • Calling attention to the output of tools available to both the author and the reviewer

In all cases, the human reviewer must have personally read and analyzed the document being reviewed, read the relevant portions of key references used to frame the review or clarify the document being reviewed, and reviewed and approved all content they are submitting under their own name.

Note that all reviews are presented to the IETF as the expert opinion of the stated reviewer. Thus, reviewers have personal responsibility for any reviews presented.

Disclosure Recommendation

Reviewers who use tools in any capacity when preparing reviews are highly encouraged to include a brief disclosure such as "I used tools to assist with {grammar checking, translation, rephrasing, research}. All technical analysis and conclusions are my own."
