# Operations of Agentic Network Managment systems

## Summary

This page attempts to capture the current state and relevant pointers for desired IETF work towards operations (OPS) of agentic network management systems, specifically AI agents, but potentially also closely related components such as "MCP-gateways".

This work started from a question to the community via a side meeting by OPS AD
