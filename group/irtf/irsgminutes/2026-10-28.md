### Re-thinking IAB reviews of IRTF RGs
Dirk mentioned that he has been discussing with the IAB how to make the reviews more productive. We typically explain the background of the research group, what it is currently doing, and its outlook for the future. He had the impression that this is hard for group chairs because they feel they are under scrutiny. How can we make this more useful for everyone? Let’s focus on research and impact and less on reporting, and perhaps share more of the reporting information ahead of the meeting. He is discussing this with Dhruv and welcomes other ideas. The IAB also organizes workshops and programs; if something comes out of our research groups, could the IAB pick it up? He will start an email thread and aim to discuss it in San Francisco.

Colin mentioned that the conversation will be about expectation setting.

Dirk added that it’s more of a dialogue: how can we focus on more interesting insights and topics and less on the formal parts? As Lixia said, agentic AI is a good topic.

Lixia mentioned that we aren’t justifying our existence but showing how the relationship is mutually beneficial.

Dirk mentioned that this is a discussion we could offer. It sounds more like a dedicated internal workshop. The IAB is usually booked during the week, but believes it is needed and would be useful for the IAB. We could discuss it more when we meet in San Francisco. It needs more time to plan, but imagines a discussion starting after San Francisco.

Lixia added that a joint conversation on this topic could strengthen our relationship with the IAB.

Dirk suggestioned that an option could be to use the next slot with the IAB to bring this up, share our thinking so far, and discuss how to have a more substantial conversation in the future. Dirk will talk to some people and update when he knows more.
